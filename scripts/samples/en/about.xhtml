About this text

"A Word at a Time" was written for FastReader and placed in the public domain under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication. You may copy it, change it, translate it or build on it, for any purpose, without asking and without giving credit.

It is here so that a new reader has something to read straight away. Nothing was downloaded to show it to you, it was not added to your library, and it left no file on your device.

The Spanish sample, "Una palabra a la vez", is the same story, written for FastReader in Spanish and released under the same dedication.
