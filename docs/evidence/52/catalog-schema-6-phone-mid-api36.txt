--- the migration, on the same device ---

The document above was then edited to schemaVersion 5 with `libraryOrder`
removed, `chapterPauseEnabled` set to false and the theme set to DARK — a
v1.1.0-shaped document — and written back over the app's catalog while the
process was stopped. On the next launch the app read it back as:

  schemaVersion ........... 6
  settings.libraryOrder ... "RECENTLY_READ"     (written by the migration step)
  settings.chapterPauseEnabled ... false        (the reader's choice, kept)
  settings.theme .......... "DARK"              (the reader's choice, kept)
