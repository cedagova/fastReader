fastReader 1.6.0 (versionCode 8) — Android 8.0+.

Install it over 1.5.0 or earlier; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place
in each of them and your settings are kept.

## New

- **An optional Reader account, under Settings.** FastReader is the owner's own
  testing app for the Reader stage backend, and this release makes it one:
  **Settings → Reader account** signs in with a six-digit code emailed to you
  (a new address gets an account), with a password, or by code-based password
  recovery; shows the account's capabilities document with the request id of
  the call and fetches it again on demand; and signs out — on this device, or
  on every other device. Reading needs no account and nothing about it
  changes: signed out, or with no network at all, FastReader is the same app as
  1.5.0 outside that one screen.

## Changed

- **FastReader now holds the internet permission.** It is used for the Reader
  account and for nothing else. The statement below says what leaves the
  device when you use the account and to whom; nothing about your books does.
  The release gate now proves that the APK's permissions are exactly the
  internet permission plus Android's own broadcast self-permission, and that
  no cleartext connection is allowed.
- **Check for updates** still only hands the releases page to your browser;
  its wording no longer says the app never connects, because the account can.

## Unchanged

Books, positions and settings survive the update. Reading itself keeps the same
cues, the same chapter pause and the same Spanish interface. The account
session, when there is one, is kept encrypted in a place the device's backup
and phone-to-phone transfer never read.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## What stays on this device

Rewritten for the account. The first four sentences are new; the rest is the
1.5.0 statement.

<!-- privacy-statement:begin -->
FastReader has the internet permission and uses it for one thing only: the
optional Reader account under Settings. Nothing is sent unless you use that
account. When you do, your email address, the code or password you type and
the account's session go to the Reader identity provider and the Reader API,
and nothing else does: your books, your reading positions, your settings and
any crash report stay on this device and are never sent. The account session
is kept encrypted on this device, outside its backup, and is removed when you
sign out. Check for updates only hands a web address to your browser, and your
browser makes that request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this
device FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings and small cover thumbnails, in its private storage. If the app stops
unexpectedly it also keeps one short report about what went wrong in that
private storage: the app version, this device's model, its Android version and
where in the code it stopped, with no part of any book in it — the next launch
offers that report to you once, and it goes nowhere unless you share it and
pick an app to send it to. None of that is included in this device's backup or
in a transfer to a new phone, so a reinstall or a new phone starts with an
empty library. When another app opens a book in FastReader and does not give
lasting permission to read it, that book is not added to your list and no
permission to it is kept; only your place in it is remembered.
<!-- privacy-statement:end -->
