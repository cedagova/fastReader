fastReader 1.4.0 (versionCode 6) — Android 8.0+, ~2.0 MB.

Install it over 1.3.0 or earlier; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place
in each of them and your settings are kept.

## Changed

- **The speed you set is now your average speed.** Until now the number on the
  dial was the speed of the plain words, and the pauses at commas, full stops
  and paragraph ends were added on top, so a book at "300" really averaged
  about 245 and a long sentence with no commas suddenly felt much faster than
  the rest. Now the pauses are paid for inside the speed you chose: a book at
  300 takes exactly as long as its word count at 300 says, and a comma-rich
  passage and an unpunctuated one feel like the same speed. **Your dial keeps
  the number you set, which now reads faster than it did.** If it feels too
  quick, turn it down: about 15% lower reproduces the old average, which at
  300 is two notches of the slider (250).
- **A pause is as long as what it closes.** A full stop after a three-word
  sentence no longer holds as long as one after a twelve-word sentence. Long
  sentences keep the full pause; choppy dialogue stops crawling.
- **Long sentences without commas get a breath.** After eight or so words with
  no punctuation, the word before an *and*, *but*, *that*, *y*, *pero*, *que*
  and the like is held a little longer, and never more than fourteen words run
  without a rest. The pause-strength setting scales these too, and *Off*
  removes them along with every other pause.
- **Time remaining is now exact.** It is the words left divided by your speed,
  and changing the pause strength no longer changes it: the strength moves time
  between pauses and words, not the total.
- **The settings preview says the same thing the reader does.** Its 250 WPM
  is an average too, and its sample sentence was lengthened so the pause it
  demonstrates stays a full one.

## Unchanged

Books, positions and settings survive the update. Reading itself keeps the same
cues, the same chapter pause and the same Spanish interface. Nothing new is
stored.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## What stays on this device

Unchanged from 1.3.0.

<!-- privacy-statement:begin -->
FastReader has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything
itself. Check for updates only hands a web address to your browser, and your
browser makes that request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this
device FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings and small cover thumbnails, in its private storage. If the app stops
unexpectedly it also keeps one short report about what went wrong in that
private storage: the app version, this device's model, its Android version and
where in the code it stopped, with no part of any book in it — the next launch
offers that report to you once, and it goes nowhere unless you share it and
pick an app to send it to. None of that is included in this device's backup or
in a transfer to a new phone, so a reinstall or a new phone starts with an
empty library. When another app opens a book in FastReader and does not give
lasting permission to read it, that book is not added to your list and no
permission to it is kept; only your place in it is remembered.
