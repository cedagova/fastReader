fastReader 1.1.0 (versionCode 3) — Android 8.0+, ~1.7 MB.

Install it over 1.0.1; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place in each of
them and your settings are kept.

## New

- **Two sample texts come with the app.** A fresh library offers a short English
  and a short Spanish text, so you can see the word stream before adding a book.
  Nothing is downloaded and nothing is added to your library.
- **Open a book from another app.** "Open with" and the share sheet now hand an
  EPUB straight to the reader. The book joins your library only when the app
  that sent it grants lasting permission to read it; otherwise it is read for
  that session and leaves nothing behind.
- **Change speed without leaving the stream.** In focused mode, drag up or down
  to move the pace in 25 WPM steps.
- **See and manage your folders.** The library shows how many folders you have
  added; removing one asks first, says how many books will leave, and can be
  undone. No file is ever deleted or changed.
- **About this reader.** Settings now shows the version and build number, a
  "Check for updates" button that opens the releases page in your browser, and
  the statement below about what stays on this device.
- **A launcher icon of its own**, and a first frame that already carries your
  chosen theme instead of flashing the wrong one.
- **A README** on the repository page, with screenshots, install steps and the
  same privacy statement.

## Fixed

- Opening a large illustrated book no longer reads its images. Opening time
  follows a book's text, not its file size.
- Your reading position, settings and library are excluded from this device's
  backup and from a phone-to-phone transfer, which is what the statement below
  promises. Before this release, a device transfer copied them.

## Unchanged

The reading experience itself is the same as 1.0.1. Words are centered by
default, one letter is highlighted, the guide marks are drawn, and the
off-center **Fixed focus letter** alignment is still an opt-in toggle that ships
off. If you turned it on in 1.0.1, it is still on after this update.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## What stays on this device

<!-- privacy-statement:begin -->
FastReader has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything
itself. Check for updates only hands a web address to your browser, and your
browser makes that request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this
device FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings and small cover thumbnails, in its private storage. None of that is
included in this device's backup or in a transfer to a new phone, so a reinstall
or a new phone starts with an empty library. When another app opens a book in
FastReader and does not give lasting permission to read it, that book is not
added to your list and no permission to it is kept; only your place in it is
remembered.
