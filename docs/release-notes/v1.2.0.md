fastReader 1.2.0 (versionCode 4) — Android 8.0+, ~2.0 MB.

Install it over 1.1.0; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place in each of
them and your settings are kept, and the two new settings start at the values
that reproduce what 1.1.0 did.

## New

- **The whole app in Spanish.** Every screen, every button, every TalkBack
  label and the privacy statement follow your phone's language. Set the phone —
  or FastReader alone, in Android's app language settings — to Spanish and the
  app is Spanish; nothing to choose inside the app.
- **Pause at chapters.** The stream stops on the first word of each new chapter
  and names it, which is what 1.1.0 always did — but it is now a setting you
  can turn off to read straight through. It ships **on**, so nothing changes
  unless you change it.
- **Skip the cover and title pages.** A book that opens on its front matter
  offers, once, to start at the first real chapter instead. Say "Start at the
  beginning" and it never asks again for that book.
- **Sort your library.** *Title*, *Recently read* or *Recently added*, from the
  button above the list. It starts at **Recently read**, so the book you are in
  the middle of is at the top.
- **Books you added elsewhere show up sooner.** Returning to the app rescans
  your folders if it has been more than a minute, instead of waiting much
  longer; pull to refresh still forces it, and a skipped rescan no longer
  flashes the scanning banner.
- **Room to read on a tablet, and in landscape.** At tablet width and whenever
  the screen is wider than it is tall, the reader puts its controls beside the
  stream instead of under it, and the library uses the extra width.
- **If FastReader stops unexpectedly, it can tell you why.** The next launch
  offers a short report: app version, device model, Android version, and where
  in the code it stopped. Nothing is sent — you either share it to an app you
  pick, or delete it, and either way you are asked once. The report holds no
  part of any book, no titles, no file names and no paths.

## Fixed

- **A book you replaced no longer drops you in the wrong place.** FastReader
  now remembers the shape of each book's archive, so editing or re-exporting a
  file under the same name is noticed and your saved position is checked
  against the new text instead of applied blindly. Books are unaffected until
  the first time you open them after this update, and opening is no slower: the
  check reads the archive's index, which was already being read.

## Unchanged

The reading experience itself is the same as 1.1.0. Words are centered by
default, one letter is highlighted, the guide marks are drawn, and the
off-center **Fixed focus letter** alignment is still an opt-in toggle that
ships off. Every setting you had is still set the way you left it.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## Under the hood

Gradle, the Android Gradle plugin, Kotlin, Compose and the AndroidX libraries
moved to current stable, and the app now compiles and targets API 37
(Android 17). The oldest phone it runs on is unchanged: Android 8.0, API 26.

## What stays on this device

The statement below has one new sentence: 1.2.0 is the first release that keeps
a crash report, so the statement now says where it lives and that it is yours
to send or delete.

<!-- privacy-statement:begin -->
FastReader has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything
itself. Check for updates only hands a web address to your browser, and your
browser makes that request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this
device FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings and small cover thumbnails, in its private storage. If the app stops
unexpectedly it also keeps one short report about what went wrong in that
private storage: the app version, this device's model, its Android version and
where in the code it stopped, with no part of any book in it — the next launch
offers that report to you once, and it goes nowhere unless you share it and
pick an app to send it to. None of that is included in this device's backup or
in a transfer to a new phone, so a reinstall or a new phone starts with an
empty library. When another app opens a book in FastReader and does not give
lasting permission to read it, that book is not added to your list and no
permission to it is kept; only your place in it is remembered.
<!-- privacy-statement:end -->
