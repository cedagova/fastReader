fastReader 1.3.0 (versionCode 5) — Android 8.0+, ~2.0 MB.

Install it over 1.2.0 or 1.1.0; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place
in each of them and your settings are kept. The one new setting starts at the
value that reproduces what you had.

## New

- **Type any reading speed.** The slider still moves in 25 WPM steps. Press the
  WPM readout beside it and it becomes a number field: type any whole number
  from 100 to 1000 and press Done. Out of range shows red and is not applied;
  dismissing the keyboard keeps a valid value and drops an invalid one.
- **Word size and text size are separate.** *Word size* is the streamed word
  alone. *Text size* is everything else: menus, lists, the paused paragraph.
  Until now one setting did both. Your word keeps the size it had; only the
  settings screen gains a row.
- **Back from the library goes to your book.** When there is a book you were
  reading, Back in the library opens it instead of leaving the app. Leave with
  Home or the app switcher, as with any reader.
- **Removing a book asks first.** The question names the book and says the file
  and your place are kept. Undo is still offered after a yes.
- **The settings preview shows the whole alphabet.** Its stream now ends with
  "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog", so every letter goes past in
  the settings you are choosing.

## Changed

- **The chapter pause says what to press.** The chapter screen now reads
  "Press ▶ to continue reading."
- **The 450 WPM comprehension note is gone.** How fast to read is your call.
- **The bundled sample texts are gone.** The empty library and the settings
  screen no longer offer them; add your own EPUB to start reading.

## Unchanged

Reading itself is as in 1.2.0: the same cues, the same rhythm, the same chapter
pause and the same Spanish interface. Every setting you had is still set the
way you left it, and books added in 1.2.0 resume where they were.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## What stays on this device

Unchanged from 1.2.0.

<!-- privacy-statement:begin -->
FastReader has no internet permission, so it cannot send or receive anything
itself. Check for updates only hands a web address to your browser, and your
browser makes that request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this
device FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings and small cover thumbnails, in its private storage. If the app stops
unexpectedly it also keeps one short report about what went wrong in that
private storage: the app version, this device's model, its Android version and
where in the code it stopped, with no part of any book in it — the next launch
offers that report to you once, and it goes nowhere unless you share it and
pick an app to send it to. None of that is included in this device's backup or
in a transfer to a new phone, so a reinstall or a new phone starts with an
empty library. When another app opens a book in FastReader and does not give
lasting permission to read it, that book is not added to your list and no
permission to it is kept; only your place in it is remembered.
