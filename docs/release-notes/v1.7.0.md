fastReader 1.7.0 (versionCode 9) — Android 8.0+.

Install it over 1.6.0 or earlier; do not uninstall first. Your books, your place
in each of them and your settings are kept.

## New

- **Your Reader account's books appear on the shelf.** Sign in under
  **Settings → Reader account** and the shelf shows what that account holds
  alongside what is on this phone. A book the account has *and* this device has
  is **one row**, not two — matched by the file's own checksum, showing the
  device's cover, author and your place in it. A book only the account has is an
  **account-only row**: title, author and "In your Reader account. Not on this
  device." It has no cover and does not open, because this release does not
  download account books to this phone.
- **Remove a book from your account, with Undo.** The account-only row offers
  **Remove from account**; the confirmation says plainly that the book leaves
  the Reader account on **every device signed in to it**, and that the file here
  and your place in it are untouched. An Undo bar follows for about eight
  seconds — the same window a device removal already gives you — and taking the
  removal back reaches the backend too.
- **Opening and finishing an account book is recorded for the account.** For a
  book your account already holds, FastReader tells the Reader API that you
  opened it, when you last opened it, and when you finished it. Nothing is
  recorded for a book that is only on this phone.
- **Add a book on this phone to your account.** A device book now offers **Add
  to account library**. It asks before anything happens, and the question says
  what it is asking for: the file's bytes go to your Reader account and are
  kept there. It shows how large the file is and the largest your account
  accepts — both read from the backend, neither built into the app. Until you
  tap **Add**, nothing about that book has left the phone. The upload then runs
  on the row with a progress bar and its own Cancel; when it finishes, the book
  appears as an account book and can be read on your other devices. Killing the
  app part-way through picks the same upload back up rather than starting a
  second one, and adding the same file twice does not make a second book. A
  refusal — too large, unsupported, copy-protected — shows the backend's own
  words and leaves the book on this phone exactly as it was.
- **One refresh control for both halves.** Pull the shelf's refresh and it
  rescans your folders *and* asks the account for its current list.

## Changed

- **The statement below says what now leaves this device.** Since 1.6.0 it said
  your books, positions and settings "stay on this device and are never sent".
  That is no longer the whole truth: your account's book list, and the reading
  status and last-opened time of the books it holds, go to the Reader API. The
  statement now says exactly that and no more — no book file is sent, a book
  only on this phone is never named to the Reader API, and your reading
  positions, reading speed, other settings and crash reports still never leave.
  It also says the one new thing FastReader keeps *here*: a copy of your
  account's book list, in the app's private storage — and that signing out does
  **not** delete it, so signing back in to the same account picks up where it
  left off. Only uninstalling FastReader or clearing its data removes it.
- **And it now says what an upload sends.** Two promises are retired with the
  add-to-account action, because they stopped being true: "no book file is ever
  sent" and "a book that is only on this device is never named to the Reader
  API". Both are replaced by the same claim made conditional on the thing the
  app actually requires — a book file is sent only for a book **you** add that
  way, and a book only on this phone is never named to the Reader API **until
  you add it**. The statement also names what adding sends besides the file:
  its name, its size, its format and its checksum. Nothing else changed: no
  reading position, no reading speed, no setting and no crash report leaves.
- **Signed out, nothing changed.** With no Reader account signed in, the shelf,
  Settings and the reader are the 1.6.0 app, and the account library sends
  nothing at all.

## Unchanged

Books, positions and settings survive the update. Reading itself keeps the same
cues, the same chapter pause and the same Spanish interface. The account
session, when there is one, is still kept encrypted in a place the device's
backup and phone-to-phone transfer never read. The release gate still proves
that the APK asks for exactly the internet permission plus Android's own
broadcast self-permission, and that no cleartext connection is allowed.

FastReader is for personal use and distributed by link. It is not on any store —
see issue #33 for why that is still true.

## What stays on this device

Rewritten for the account library and for adding a book to it. The sentences
about what the Reader API is told, and about what an upload sends, are new; the
rest is the 1.6.0 statement.

FastReader has the internet permission and uses it for one thing only:
the optional Reader account under Settings. Nothing is sent unless you
use that account. When you do, your email address, the code or password
you type and the account's session go to the Reader identity provider
and the Reader API. FastReader also asks the Reader API which books your
account already holds, and for those books only it tells the Reader API
that you opened one, when you last opened it, whether you have finished
it, and when you take one out of your account or put it back. When you
choose Add to account library for a book on this device and confirm,
FastReader asks the Reader API what kinds and sizes of file your account
accepts and then sends that book's file, its name, its size, its format
and its checksum to the Reader API and its storage, where your account
keeps them; nothing about that book is sent before you confirm. That is
all that leaves this device: a book file is sent only for a book you add
that way, a book that is only on this device is never named to the
Reader API until you add it, and your reading positions, your reading
speed, your other settings and any crash report stay on this device and
are never sent. The account session is kept encrypted on this device,
outside its backup, and is removed when you sign out. Check for updates
only hands a web address to your browser, and your browser makes that
request. Your books stay in the folders you chose; on this device
FastReader keeps only its own list of them, your reading positions, your
settings, small cover thumbnails and, once you sign in, a copy of your
account's own book list and a note of any book you are part-way through
adding to it, in its private storage. That copy of the account's list is
not deleted when you sign out: it stays in that private storage, so
signing in to the same account again picks up where it left off, and
only uninstalling FastReader or clearing its data removes it. If the app
stops unexpectedly it also keeps one short report about what went wrong
in that private storage: the app version, this device's model, its
Android version and where in the code it stopped, with no part of any
book in it — the next launch offers that report to you once, and it goes
nowhere unless you share it and pick an app to send it to. None of that
is included in this device's backup or in a transfer to a new phone, so
a reinstall or a new phone starts with an empty library. When another
app opens a book in FastReader and does not give lasting permission to
read it, that book is not added to your list and no permission to it is
kept; only your place in it is remembered.
